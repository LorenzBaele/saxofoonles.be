Which details?

What do I use them for?

To answer your question, arrange lessons and send invoices. I can do this because you contact me yourself to take lessons. I have to keep invoices because the law requires it.

The form goes through Web3Forms, which sends your message to my Gmail inbox. Apart from that, I don't share your details with anyone, unless the law requires it.

How long do I keep them?

For as long as I need them for your question or your lessons. I keep invoices for as long as the law requires.

This website doesn't use cookies and doesn't keep visitor statistics.

Your rights

You can always view, correct or delete your details via lorenzbaele.booking@gmail.com, and file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority.
