Frequently asked questions

I give private saxophone lessons in Ghent, to children and adults, for every level. Here you'll find the answers to the most common questions.

How much does a saxophone lesson cost?

A 30-minute lesson costs €30, a 1-hour lesson costs €55. If I come to your home, travel costs are added at the Belgian mileage rate.

Can I start without any experience?

Yes, of course. You don't need any experience and you don't need to read music. We start with the basics: putting your saxophone together, holding it properly and playing your first notes. I'll teach you to read music along the way, if you'd like.

Do I need my own saxophone?

No. You can rent a saxophone from me, per semester or per school year. You keep it at home, so you can practise every day.

Can I take a trial lesson first?

Yes. Your first lesson is a regular lesson at the regular price, with no commitment. Afterwards you decide whether you want to continue, and how often.

Do you also teach children?

Yes, I teach children, teenagers and adults. There's no fixed minimum age: it depends on the child, for example whether they can hold the saxophone properly and have enough breath. Not sure? Send me a message and we'll look at it together.

Where do the lessons take place?

In my studio in Ghent, at your home or online. For lessons at home, I come to Ghent and the surrounding area. I charge travel costs at the Belgian mileage rate.

Can I take lessons online?

Yes, by video call. Handy if you live a bit further away or don't have much time to travel.

Which saxophones can I learn to play?

All four: alto, tenor, soprano and baritone. Not sure yet which one you want? Then we'll choose one together.

Which music styles can I learn?

Whatever you like to play: pop and rock, jazz, funk and soul, or classical. We'll pick the songs you're excited about together.

Do I need to speak Dutch?

No. I teach in English as well as in Dutch, in my studio in Ghent, at your home or online, for children and adults of every level.
